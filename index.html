About NUB Film & Photography Club

NUB Film & Photography Club is a creative community of passionate students at Northern University Bangladesh who love capturing stories through cameras and visual arts. The club provides a platform for students to explore photography, cinematography, and filmmaking while expressing their creativity and unique perspectives. Our mission is to inspire young creators to see the world through a different lens and transform ordinary moments into powerful visual stories. Through workshops, photo walks, film screenings, and collaborative projects, members gain practical skills and experience in photography and filmmaking. The club actively covers university events, organizes photography exhibitions, competitions, and creative campaigns that encourage students to showcase their talent and develop their artistic vision.

  • 📷 Photography & Videography Training
  • 🎬 Short Film & Cinematography Projects
  • 🚶 Photo Walks & Outdoor Shoots
  • 🚶 🖼️ Photography Exhibitions & Competitions
  • 🎓 Workshops, Seminars & Creative Discussions

Our Activities

How We Help You

🎥 Videography

Our Videography team focuses on capturing stories through motion and cinematic visuals. From university events and cultural programs to creative short videos, we work to transform moments into engaging visual content. Members learn the full process of video production, including camera operation, shot composition, lighting techniques, and audio recording. We also explore storytelling methods, helping creators plan scenes, frame shots, and build narratives that connect with the audience. The club encourages students to experiment with different styles of videography such as event coverage, documentaries, promotional videos, and short cinematic projects. Through teamwork and hands-on practice, members develop both technical skills and creative vision while producing high-quality visual content for the university community. 🎬📹

📷 Photography

Photography is one of the core creative activities of the club, where members learn to capture moments, emotions, and stories through the lens. From portraits and street photography to nature and event photography, the club encourages students to explore different styles and develop their own visual perspective. Members get the opportunity to learn camera settings, composition techniques, lighting control, and creative framing. Through regular practice sessions, photo walks, and collaborative projects, photographers improve both their technical skills and artistic vision. The club also covers university programs and special events, allowing members to gain real-world experience while building a strong photography portfolio. Our goal is to help passionate photographers turn simple moments into powerful visual stories. 📸

🎓 Workshops & Training

The club regularly organizes workshops and training sessions to help members develop both creative and technical skills in photography and filmmaking. These sessions are designed for beginners as well as experienced members who want to improve their craft. Participants learn important topics such as camera basics, composition, lighting techniques, storytelling, and editing tools used in modern photography and videography. Experienced members and guest professionals often share practical knowledge, tips, and real-world experiences to guide new creators. Through hands-on practice, interactive sessions, and collaborative learning, members gain confidence behind the camera and develop the skills needed to create high-quality visual content. The goal of these workshops is to inspire creativity, encourage experimentation, and help students grow as visual storytellers..

🎉 Event Coverage

The club provides professional photography and videography coverage for various university events, including seminars, cultural programs, competitions, and special celebrations. Our team works to capture the most memorable moments and important highlights of every event. Members get the opportunity to gain real-world experience by working in live event environments, learning how to capture candid moments, stage performances, and audience interactions. From behind-the-scenes shots to key event highlights, we focus on telling the complete story of each program. Through event coverage, members not only improve their technical skills but also learn teamwork, planning, and creative storytelling while representing the university through high-quality visual content. 📸🎥🎉
